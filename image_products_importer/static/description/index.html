Product Image Importer

Bulk import product images by matching filenames to product references, names, or barcodes. This app enables seamless and fast import of large batches of product images, ensuring accurate matching of images to the respective products in your inventory.

Features include:

  • Bulk image import for multiple products at once
  • Support for case-insensitive matching and handling of underscores and dashes in filenames
  • Flexible matching based on product reference, name, or barcode
  • Batch processing for efficiency with large datasets
  • Easy integration into your existing Odoo inventory system
