Bulk Image Importer

Import hundreds of product images into Odoo in just a few clicks! Our Bulk Image Importer matches filenames with products using:

  • Internal Reference (SKU)
  • Product Name
  • Barcode

The importer is case-insensitive and ignores dashes/underscores, making matching reliable and simple.

Whether you have 10 or 10,000 products, this app saves hours of manual work and ensures your catalog is always up to date.
